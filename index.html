    01 — Divine Bond

    A Bond Written in Time

    One of the most beloved stories associated with the spirit of Raksha Bandhan is the bond between Krishna and Draupadi.

    In the stories passed down through generations, they shared a bond that went beyond ordinary friendship.

    When Draupadi needed Krishna...

    Krishna stood beside her.

    And when Krishna needed love...

    Draupadi's affection reminded us that protection is never only about strength.

    It is about being there.

    A brother's promise is not simply...

    "I will protect you."

    It is...

    "I will stand beside you."

    Through every chapter.

    Thousands of years may separate our stories...

    But the feeling hasn't changed.

    Today, that story is ours.
